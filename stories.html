October 2025

School fees paid for Mawia Sammy

Ukasi Compassionate Heart Foundation Chairman, Patrick Kitonga, together with members including Reverend Moses Muthui, paid school fees for Mawia Sammy at St. Teresa Girls.

October 2025

A message of gratitude from Musili Karani

"I would like to express my heartfelt gratitude to Ukasi Compassionate Heart Foundation for your generous support in paying my high school fees. Your kindness has lifted a great burden from my family and given me the opportunity to focus fully on my education." — Musili Karani

2025

Jackson Kiema's story

From Mwingi Central, Jackson completed Form Four but lacked university fees — until UCHF stepped in. He earned a Bachelor's degree in Education Science, specialising in physics and chemistry, and was employed by the Teachers Service Commission, posted in Garissa town.

2025

"Much gratitude to the foundation"

"May God enable them to reach more vulnerable students in every part of this country and even around the globe. Am sincerely grateful, may Almighty God bless Ukasi Compassionate Heart Foundation abundantly." — Jackson Kiema

2020

A Story of Hope

Nine bright, vulnerable students were kept in school: Felix Muthui (Kyuso Boys), Rhoda Mutisya (Yambyu Girls), Emma Kiema and Ali Esther Mueni (Kyondoni Girls), Peter Kitheka and Samson Mwandi (Ngomeni Boys), Joyce Kimuti (St. Teresa's Girls), Joseph Mui (Kyuso Boys), and Elizabeth Kakii, a pupil at Ngaaie Special School who touched many hearts with her determination despite her special needs.

November 2018

Extending a Hand of Hope

Financial assistance reached students across many schools: Judy Wamtu, Innocent Nzioka and Susan Kitonga (St. Teresa's Girls), Musila Muthui (Kisasi Boys), Mwangangi Maweu and Eric Juma (Mwingi Boys), Mulei Mwiru (Ngomeni Boys), Dominic Muthui (Nuu Boys), Grace Kimanzi (St. Angela's Girls), Kanyiva Mawia (Ndatani Girls), Gloria Nduku (Jordan College, Thika), Elizabeth Kakii (Maii School for the Blind), David Mumbo (Mwingi West Teachers Training College) and Alex Mailu (Kalonzo Secondary). Mr. Enoch Nguthu, through UCHF, fully sponsored Mwoki Mutua's fees.

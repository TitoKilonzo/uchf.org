Welcome to UCHF

Your support keeps a child in class.

This foundation was founded in August 2015 by community members who were moved by compassion to assist the needy and most vulnerable children in Ukasi. What began as neighbours pooling resources has continued, year after year, as a practical, non-political effort to keep bright students in school.

You can be part of what we are doing by supporting this cause financially. Your support goes a long way toward having a student in class — and toward shaping their future.

Read our background

Our ethos

One day, every child here will have the chance at an excellent education — regardless of family background.

That's the vision the founding members wrote down in 2015, and it still sets the direction for every fee payment, every partnership and every volunteer hour UCHF puts in.

Our mission

We are committed to mobilizing resources from within and outside the community to support the needy and most vulnerable children in education and development.

Core values

What we hold ourselves to

  • 01Compassion — every decision starts with the student in front of us.
  • 02Integrity — doing the right thing, especially when no one is watching.
  • 03Honesty — clear, truthful communication with students, families and donors.
  • 04Commitment — staying with a student's education from enrolment to graduation.
  • 05Accountability — every shilling given is a shilling we account for.

Goals & objectives

How we work toward that vision

  • Enlighten and mobilize the community resourcefully to invest in education, offering incentives where we can.
  • Create linkages between the community and other actors working in education and development.
  • Build a network of like-minded people to foster the exchange of ideas.
  • Serve as a social-audit provider for community development and projects.

Foundation context

Non-political, but practical.

Our primary objective is to promote education through active, continuous engagement with needy students. Our volunteers network and coordinate responses and interventions toward education and development advancement in Ukasi Community — not as a competitor to other efforts, but as a complement to them.

Home / About Us

We are on a journey back to the community.

Founded by neighbours in Ukasi who decided that a child's future shouldn't depend on their family's bank balance.

Vision

One day, all children in this community will have the opportunity to attain an excellent education — irrespective of their family background.

Mission

Mobilizing resources, inside and outside the community, for education and development.

We are committed to mobilizing resources from within and outside the community to support the needy and most vulnerable children in education and development. That means paying fees where we can, and coordinating with schools, families and partners to make sure support lands where it's needed most.

Our background

Founded August 2015

This foundation was founded in August 2015 by community members who were moved by compassion to assist the needy and most vulnerable children in Ukasi. The founders unanimously agreed to provide a setting inspired by shared vision, unity of purpose, and the belief that the destiny of young people is not tied to the current circumstances of their parents and community.

Members agreed to pool together resources and ideas to assist secondary and post-secondary education for the most needy students. The founding members proposed, adopted and gave themselves a constitution — the force of law guiding the validity and legality of every activity and every member's conduct.
